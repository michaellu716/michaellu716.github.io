BMO: Adventure Time AI Bot

A 3D-printed BMO from Adventure Time, powered by a local LLM.

Personal project · Hardware + AI · 2026

2 weeks
To print and build
Local
LLM on-device

The software is written in Python: Ollama runs the language model locally, openWakeWord enables hands-free wake-word activation, Moondream adds on-device computer vision, and DuckDuckGo powers smart web search.
